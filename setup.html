Cards

Print first, decide later. Add a card per sticker you want to carry and publish — every sticker prints the same, so nothing is committed to a place yet. Out walking: tape one up, find its card by the code printed on it, then tap 📍 Use my location, name the spot and pick a picture. Publish again when you're home. Outdoorsy pictures get grass and trees drawn around them on the map.

Missing-sticker emails

When a kid taps 🙈 Sticker Missing? Report it! we email you so you can reprint that sign. Tap below to check the email actually works — it skips the one-per-stop-per-12-hours limit.

Print the QR codes

Every sticker is the same generic Neighborhood Quest card — no place name on it, so you can tape any one anywhere. Cut them out, laminate if you like, and carry them on your walk. The big code is what a kid types when their camera won't cooperate, and what tells you which card above belongs to the sticker in your hand.

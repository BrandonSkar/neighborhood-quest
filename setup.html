🗺️ Neighborhood Quest — Setup

Add a card for each printout, drop its exact pin, then publish. The warmer 🔥 / colder ❄️ hunt points at wherever you drop it, on every kid's phone.

Cards

Stand where the printout will go and tap 📍 Use my location on that card — that's the pin the kids' warmer/colder hunt points at. Tap the picture to change it; outdoorsy pictures also get grass and trees drawn around them on the map.

Off by default, so publishing only updates the cards and nobody loses progress. Tick it when you've moved to a whole new set of spots.

Publishing saves your cards to the game's database so every phone loads them next time it opens (online). The game still works offline after that.

Print the QR codes

One code per card. Cut them out, laminate if you like, and tape each at its dropped-pin spot. Each sheet also prints the typed code, so a kid whose camera won't cooperate can enter it in the app instead.

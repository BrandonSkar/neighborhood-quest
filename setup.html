🗺️ Neighborhood Quest — Setup

1. Print a sheet of blank stickers.  2. Tape one somewhere good.  3. Type its code in here, drop the pin and publish.  🎁 Prize →  📊 Dashboard →

1. Print a sheet of stickers

Nine blank Neighborhood Quest stickers, three across, each with its own code. No place names — nothing is committed to a spot yet, so you can tape any one of them anywhere. The nine codes are all different from each other and from every card you've already published, so a new sticker can never clash with a live one. Cut them out, laminate if you like, and take them for a walk.

The sheet itself waits at the bottom of this page — printing sends the stickers and nothing else.

2. Areas

An area is one park's worth of hunt. Each one runs on its own: only its 🚩 start sticker shows on the map until a child scans it, then the rest of that area's stickers appear, and once they've found them all a 🎁 treasure chest turns up — bigger and gold — with a real prize hidden at it. Three parks means three areas and three prizes. Add stickers to an area whenever you like; it just means one more to find before the chest.

3. Stickers you've placed

Standing at a sticker you just taped up? Type the code printed on it, then tap 📍 Use my location, name the spot, pick a picture and add a question. One sticker at a time — publish whenever you like.

4. Publish

Publishing never resets anybody. A stamp belongs to the sticker's code, so adding a sticker to a park somebody has already finished just puts one more pin on their map to go back for — nothing they've found is ever taken away.

Publishing saves your cards to the game's database so every phone loads them next time it opens (online). The game still works offline after that.

Start over

Clears every scan, player and missing-sticker tally from your database — the dashboard goes back to zero. This can't be undone. It does not reach the kids' phones: their stamps live in their own browsers, and each of them clears their own with 🗑️ Delete my data in the app.

Alerts

A notification on your phone every time a kid scans a sticker — and when one taps 🙈 Sticker Missing? Report it!. Free, and only phones where you tap this ever buzz.

The test skips the one-per-stop-per-12-hours limit and reports what every channel did. Setup steps live on the 🎁 Prize page.

Put this page on your phone as its own app. iPhone: open it in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen — that's the only way Apple allows a web page to send notifications at all (iOS 16.4+). Android: Chrome's ⋮ menu → Install app. It lands as Quest Setup, opens without browser chrome, and is separate from the kids' game — you don't need that one installed.

🗺️ Neighborhood Quest — Setup

Add a card for each printout, drop its exact pin, then publish. The warmer 🔥 / colder ❄️ hunt points at wherever you drop it, on every kid's phone.

Used only to build the QR links below. The tip is: tape each QR at its spot after you drop its pin standing there.

Cards

Best way to get exact coords: stand at the printout's spot and tap 📍 Use my location on that card. Or type coordinates in by hand.

Leave this on when you change the spots. Turn it off if you're only nudging a pin and don't want to wipe anyone's progress.

Publishing saves your cards to the game's database so every phone loads them next time it opens (online). The game still works offline after that.

Print the QR codes

One code per card. Cut them out, laminate if you like, and tape each at its dropped-pin spot.

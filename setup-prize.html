🎁 Neighborhood Quest — Prize

Find enough treasures and the child gets to pick a prize — then they're shown where to collect it.  🗺️ Cards →  📊 Dashboard →

1. When does it unlock?

Turn it off any time — the child just never sees it, and nothing else changes.

2. The prizes to choose from

Photograph each prize. Pictures are shrunk on this device before they're sent, so a phone photo is fine. Leave a slot empty to offer fewer than four. The name underneath is optional.

3. Where to collect it

One photo of the hiding place, and the words the child is shown once they've picked. This is the only place your address is stored — it isn't in the app's code.

Shown exactly as you type it, under the photo.

4. Save it

Saves to the game's database. Every phone picks it up next time it opens.

What the child will see

🎁 Select your prize!

5. Getting told the moment they pick

They'll walk straight to the hiding place, so you want to hear about it in seconds. Same free phone notifications as Sparkle Quest's chore alerts — no SMS service, nothing per message.

Per phone, on purpose: only a phone where you tapped this ever buzzes. Do it on any other phone that should hear about it too. On iPhone, add this page to the Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen) and turn it on from there — Apple only allows notifications in an installed app.

One-time setup, and the other ways to be told

Notifications need a keypair. On any computer: npx web-push generate-vapid-keys, then in Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables add VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY and VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY and redeploy. (The pair from your Sparkle Quest project works fine here — subscriptions belong to the site, not the keys.)

Email RESEND_API_KEY — the one missing-sticker alerts already use.
A real text ALERT_SMS — your number at your carrier's email-to-text gateway, e.g. 2535550123@tmomail.net. Free, but carriers block these unpredictably.
ntfy NTFY_TOPIC — a topic on the free ntfy.sh app, if you'd rather not use the button above.
